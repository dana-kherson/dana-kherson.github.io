Privacy & cookies

Last updated: 1 October 2026.

This website shares Dana’s life and volunteer work in Kherson, Ukraine. For privacy questions or requests about your information, contact the site operator at info@dana-kherson.org.

Your cookie choice

Google Analytics is optional. We do not load it unless you select “Accept analytics”. Declining does not limit access to the site. Use “Cookie settings” in the footer at any time to accept or withdraw consent.

We store your choice in your browser’s local storage for 180 days. This preference is not an analytics identifier. Clearing browser storage removes your choice. If your browser blocks storage, we may ask again on your next visit.

Optional analytics

With your consent, Google Analytics processes information about visits and interactions, such as pages viewed, referral sources, browser and device information, and approximate location. Google receives technical connection information when the analytics service loads. Analytics cookies such as _ga and _ga_* help distinguish visits. We configure a 180-day cookie lifetime, which may be renewed during visits. Advertising personalization and Google signals are disabled in our website code.

Withdrawing consent stops future analytics collection from this site and removes accessible Google Analytics cookies for this domain. It does not automatically erase information already processed by Google. See Google’s Privacy Policy for details about Google’s processing, international transfers and retention.

Hosting and fonts

This site is hosted on GitHub Pages. The hosting provider receives technical information, including your IP address, when serving the site. See GitHub’s Privacy Statement. Our pages also load fonts from Google Fonts, which sends a request to Google independently of optional Analytics. See Google Fonts privacy information.

Messages and external services

If you email us, we receive your email address and the contents of your message and use them to respond and handle your request. Please do not send sensitive information unless needed. Links to Patreon, social networks and payment services take you to other providers, whose own privacy policies apply.

Your questions and requests

You can contact us to ask about information we hold about you or request access, correction or deletion. Your rights depend on applicable law and may include withdrawing consent and raising a concern with a data protection authority. We retain correspondence only as needed to handle the matter and meet applicable obligations.

Your support makes a real difference

Would you like to learn more and help us continue our work? We would be truly grateful for your support.
